Community themes

Looks for Pathem made and shared by the people who use it. A theme tints the whole window and brings its own folder style, file icons, and toolbar glyphs.

Every theme here is free to use under the license listed on its card. To install one, open Pathem, go to Settings > Appearance, and click Themes… to open the gallery. You can preview a theme on your own window before you keep it.
